# Operations Runbooks

Project-specific operational policy and expensive-run gates live here. Generic
engineering principles stay outside the repository; historical incident detail
belongs in migration/history notes or focused regression tests.

- `environment_and_running_EN.md`: canonical standalone setup, host requirements,
  AI/operator migration audit, execution, output ownership, and duration
  boundaries.
- `expensive_runs_EN.md`: current long-run policy and its revision discipline.
