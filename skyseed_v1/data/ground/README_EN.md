# Ground Data

Reusable geographic UT, gateway, zone, binding, and policy catalogs. Filenames
retain baseline identity where hashes are contract-bound; an example must state
explicitly when it reuses a catalog with another constellation shell.

The `s5_operational_clean_*` family is generated by
`tools/ground_presets/s5_operational_clean/build.py`. Its coverage report binds
the selected 9 UTs, 5 gateways, and 6 zones to a two-hour, 10-second-frame S5
analysis window. S5 does not consume the S4 UT policy or binding catalog.
