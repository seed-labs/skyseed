如何理解这份计划 / Reading this plan

区间表示这份计划的覆盖，不表示每秒都有新操作。初始状态是起点操作前的配置；新操作由本批首次返回。RoutePlan 的前批上下文仅用于还原状态，不重复成为新指令。交付批次可能因延迟尾部重叠。图中查看显示该时刻应用操作后的状态。

Coverage is an interval, not an update every second. Initial entries precede operations at the start. New operations belong to this batch; RoutePlan context restores state without redelivery. Delayed batches may overlap. The graph shows state after operations at that time.
