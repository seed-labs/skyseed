# Starlink Gen1 research

This collection consolidates reusable external evidence encountered while
building the SkySEED S1-S5 and v2 S4 scenarios:

- `shells/`: FCC Schedule S orbital records and SkySEED S1-S5 mappings;
- `ground-stations/`: regulator, operator, independent, and community gateway
  evidence plus a reproducible benchmark proposal;
- `access-capabilities/`: Gen1 Gateway/Satellite simultaneous-access evidence
  and the bounded S4 abstraction derived from it;
- `papers/`, `tools/`, and `experiments/`: lightweight indexes that prevent
  repeated source discovery.

The material is research evidence. Example inputs and accepted designs retain
their own authority boundaries, and runtime must not load this directory.
